Project Key: dictionary and context models

The program is under the MIT License. The three data files below are
distributed with it under their own licenses. They are separate files the
program reads at run time; each can be replaced or removed, and the program
falls back to McBopomofo's dictionary and ranking without them. They were made
by tools/bigram-trainer in https://github.com/KoukeNeko/Project-Key, which
rebuilds them from the sources listed here.

bigram.bin, chars.bin
  Word and character n-gram statistics counted from:
  - Chinese Wikipedia (wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh, first shard), by
    Wikipedia contributors, under CC BY-SA 4.0
    (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The text was converted
    to Taiwan Traditional Chinese with OpenCC and only counted; no text is
    included.
  - 立法院公報 (Legislative Yuan Gazette), 資料提供者：立法院, obtained through
    the g0v Legislative Yuan API (https://v2.ly.govapi.tw), under the Open
    Government Data License, version 1.0
    (https://data.gov.tw/license).
  These two files are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

sentence.bin
  The weights of a character-level GPT-2 trained by tools/lm-trainer on
  Chinese Wikipedia (wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh, first shard), by
  Wikipedia contributors, under CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), converted to Taiwan
  Traditional Chinese with OpenCC, then fine-tuned to rank the composer's
  candidate sentences as Gemma 4 E2B (google/gemma-4-E2B, by Google DeepMind,
  under the Apache License 2.0) does. The candidates were typed from:
  - the Wikipedia text above;
  - the 立法院公報 text above;
  - that text rewritten as everyday speech by Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0
    (MediaTek-Research/Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0, by MediaTek Research, under
    the Apache License 2.0).
  No text is included. This file is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

data.txt
  McBopomofo's dictionary (MIT License, see McBopomofo-LICENSE.txt), with
  words added from:
  - libchewing-data dict/chewing/tsi.csv, commit
    c44e81aef24b06f1509f19e1be54c99812d0c43f
    (https://github.com/chewing/libchewing-data), Copyright (c) 2025
    libchewing Core Team, under LGPL-2.1-or-later;
  - words found in the training text above;
  - road and district names from 全國路名資料 (national road names, year 115
    edition), 資料提供者：內政部戶政司 (https://data.gov.tw/dataset/35321), under
    the Open Government Data License, version 1.0
    (https://data.gov.tw/license).
  This file is licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later; the license text is in
  LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt. Its source form is the file itself, and
  tools/bigram-trainer/train.sh rebuilds it.

Project Key：詞庫與上下文模型

程式本身以 MIT 授權發布。以下三個資料檔隨程式散布，各有自己的授權。它們是
程式執行時讀取的獨立檔案，可以替換或移除；沒有它們時，程式改用 McBopomofo
的詞庫與排序。這些檔案由 https://github.com/KoukeNeko/Project-Key 的
tools/bigram-trainer 產生，可依下列來源重新建置。

bigram.bin、chars.bin
  從下列文字統計出的詞與字 n-gram：
  - 中文維基百科（wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh 第一個分片），維基百科貢獻
    者，CC BY-SA 4.0（https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/）。文字
    經 OpenCC 轉為臺灣正體後只做統計，不含原文。
  - 立法院公報，資料提供者：立法院，經 g0v 立法院 API
    （https://v2.ly.govapi.tw）取得，政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版
    （https://data.gov.tw/license）。
  這兩個檔案以 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權。

sentence.bin
  由 tools/lm-trainer 訓練的字元級 GPT-2 權重，訓練文字為中文維基百科
  （wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh 第一個分片），維基百科貢獻者，CC BY-SA 4.0
  （https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/），經 OpenCC 轉為臺灣正體；
  再微調，使它排序組字候選句的方式接近 Gemma 4 E2B（google/gemma-4-E2B，Google
  DeepMind，Apache License 2.0）。候選句打自：
  - 上述維基百科文字；
  - 上述立法院公報文字；
  - 由 Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0（MediaTek-Research/Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0，
    聯發創新基地，Apache License 2.0）改寫為日常口語的上述公報文字。
  不含原文。這個檔案以 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權。

data.txt
  McBopomofo 的詞庫（MIT 授權，見 McBopomofo-LICENSE.txt），另加入：
  - libchewing-data 的 dict/chewing/tsi.csv（commit
    c44e81aef24b06f1509f19e1be54c99812d0c43f，
    https://github.com/chewing/libchewing-data），Copyright (c) 2025
    libchewing Core Team，LGPL-2.1-or-later；
  - 從上述訓練文字找出的詞；
  - 全國路名資料（115 年版）的路名與鄉鎮市區名，資料提供者：內政部戶政司
    （https://data.gov.tw/dataset/35321），政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版
    （https://data.gov.tw/license）。
  這個檔案以 LGPL-2.1-or-later 授權，條文見 LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt。檔案本身
  即為原始形式，tools/bigram-trainer/train.sh 可重新建置。
