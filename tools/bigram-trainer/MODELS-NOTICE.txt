bigram.bin, chars.bin, sentence.bin
  Word and character n-gram statistics (bigram.bin, chars.bin) and character-
  level GPT-2 weights (sentence.bin) trained on a Taiwan Traditional Chinese
  corpus (~136.7M characters) composed from:
  - Taiwan Traditional Chinese web text from agentlans/fineweb2-chinese
    (TAIWAN.jsonl.zst, derived from HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-2), under the Open
    Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY).
  - Taiwan Traditional Chinese Q&A from twinkle-ai/tw-reasoning-instruct-50k,
    under the MIT License.
  - 立法院公報 (Legislative Yuan Gazette), 資料提供者：立法院, obtained through
    the g0v Legislative Yuan API (https://v2.ly.govapi.tw), under the Open
    Government Data License, version 1.0 (https://data.gov.tw/license),
    including passages rewritten as everyday Taiwanese Mandarin by
    Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0 (MediaTek-Research/Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0, by
    MediaTek Research, under the Apache License 2.0).
  - Traditional Chinese Wikinews (erhwenkuo/wikinews-zhtw), by Wikinews
    contributors, under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  - Mozilla Common Voice 25 zh-TW validated sentence transcripts
    (OpenFormosa/common_voice_25_zh-TW), under CC0 1.0.
  - Filtered Chinese Wikipedia (wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh, first shard),
    by Wikipedia contributors, under CC BY-SA 4.0
    (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), converted to Taiwan
    Traditional Chinese with OpenCC.
  No source text is included. These three files are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

data.txt
  McBopomofo's dictionary (MIT License, see McBopomofo-LICENSE.txt), with
  words added from:
  - libchewing-data dict/chewing/tsi.csv, commit
    c44e81aef24b06f1509f19e1be54c99812d0c43f
    (https://github.com/chewing/libchewing-data), Copyright (c) 2025
    libchewing Core Team, under LGPL-2.1-or-later;
  - words found in the training text;
  - road and district names from 全國路名資料 (national road names, year 115
    edition), 資料提供者：內政部戶政司 (https://data.gov.tw/dataset/35321), under
    the Open Government Data License, version 1.0
    (https://data.gov.tw/license).
  This file is licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later; the license text is in
  LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt. Its source form is the file itself, and
  tools/bigram-trainer/train.sh rebuilds it.

bigram.bin、chars.bin、sentence.bin
  由臺灣繁體中文混合語料（約 1.37 億字）訓練之詞與字元 n-gram 統計模型
  （bigram.bin、chars.bin）與字元級 GPT-2 權重（sentence.bin），來源包含：
  - 臺灣繁體中文網頁語料：agentlans/fineweb2-chinese（TAIWAN.jsonl.zst，
    衍生自 HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-2），採 ODC-BY 授權。
  - 臺灣繁體中文問答語料：twinkle-ai/tw-reasoning-instruct-50k，採 MIT 授權。
  - 立法院公報，資料提供者：立法院，經 g0v 立法院 API
    （https://v2.ly.govapi.tw）取得，採政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版
    （https://data.gov.tw/license），另包含由 Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0
    （MediaTek-Research/Breeze-7B-Instruct-v1_0，聯發創新基地，Apache
    License 2.0）改寫為臺灣日常口語之公報文本。
  - 繁體中文維基新聞（erhwenkuo/wikinews-zhtw），維基新聞貢獻者，採
    CC BY-SA 3.0 授權。
  - Mozilla Common Voice 25 臺灣華語驗證語句文字（OpenFormosa/
    common_voice_25_zh-TW），採 CC0 1.0 授權。
  - 清洗後之中文維基百科（wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.zh 第一個分片），
    維基百科貢獻者，採 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權
    （https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/），經 OpenCC 轉為臺灣
    正體。
  不含原文。這三個檔案以 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權。
