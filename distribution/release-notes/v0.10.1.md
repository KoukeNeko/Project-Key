## Bug fixes

- **Candidate order.** A reading's candidates are listed in the order the input method weighs them in context, so the word it typed is no longer listed after words that are only more common on their own. Words added by the user are still listed first, and the punctuation and symbol lists keep their order. Associated phrases are ordered by how well they follow the committed text.
- **Full-width tilde.** ～ can be typed again. Pressing ~ opens the emoji groups with ～ as the first item, picked with 1 or Enter.
- **Candidate window on more Wayland compositors (Linux).** On compositors built on Smithay, such as COSMIC, the candidate window is placed below the text instead of over it. On niri, the gap between the text and the candidate window is kept.
- **Sharper candidate window at fractional scales (Linux).** On Wayland compositors that support fractional scaling (for example 125% or 150%), the candidate window is drawn at the display's scale, so its text is no longer blurred.
- **Candidate window width (Linux).** When all candidates fit on one page, the candidate window is only as wide as its candidates.

## 錯誤修正

- **候選字順序**：同一讀音的候選字依輸入法在上下文中的判斷排序，輸入法選用的字詞不再排在單獨看來較常用的字詞之後。自訂詞仍列在最前面，標點與符號清單維持原有順序。聯想詞依接在已送出文字之後的通順程度排序。
- **全形波浪號**：可再次輸入「～」。按 ~ 開啟表情符號分類時，第一項為「～」，按 1 或 Enter 即可選取。
- **更多 Wayland 合成器的候選視窗位置（Linux）**：在 COSMIC 等以 Smithay 實作的合成器上，候選視窗置於文字下方，不再蓋住文字。在 niri 上，文字與候選視窗之間保留間距。
- **分數縮放下更清晰的候選視窗（Linux）**：在支援分數縮放（例如 125%、150%）的 Wayland 合成器上，候選視窗依螢幕的縮放比例繪製，文字不再模糊。
- **候選視窗寬度（Linux）**：候選字只有一頁時，候選視窗的寬度依候選字而定。
