## New features

- **Models in the welcome window.** The welcome window downloads the newest sentence model when one is newer than the installed version, and offers the optional advanced model (approximately 1.3 GB), which is downloaded only on request.

## Changes

- **Settings window.** The settings are divided into four pages, 選字, 外觀, 按鍵 and 關於, with a switcher at the top of the window: a segmented control on macOS, a selector bar on Windows, libadwaita's inline view switcher on GNOME (the header-bar switcher before libadwaita 1.7), and tabs on KDE. Restoring the default settings has moved to the end of the 關於 page. On GNOME, the window opens tall enough for its longest page, limited to the height of the screen.

## 繁體中文

## 新功能

- **歡迎視窗的模型**：已安裝的選字模型有較新版本時，歡迎視窗自動下載；選用的進階模型（約 1.3 GB）則於按下「下載」後才下載。

## 變更

- **設定視窗**：設定分為「選字」「外觀」「按鍵」「關於」四頁，於視窗上方切換：macOS 為分段控制項，Windows 為選取列，GNOME 為 libadwaita 的內嵌檢視切換器（libadwaita 1.7 以前改於標題列切換），KDE 為分頁標籤。「恢復原廠設定」移至「關於」頁末。GNOME 上視窗開啟時的高度足以完整顯示最長的一頁，以螢幕高度為上限。
