## Changes

↩️ Enter sends a syllable still open without a tone as its Zhuyin, since that Zhuyin is a candidate too: ㄋㄧㄏㄠ then Enter gives 你ㄏㄠ. Syllables that a later key already closed still go as their words. To send 你好, pick it from the candidates or type the tone.

## 變更

↩️ 按 Enter 時，最後一個還沒打聲調的音節會送出注音符號，因為注音本身也是候選之一：ㄋㄧㄏㄠ 按 Enter 送出「你ㄏㄠ」。已經被下一個按鍵結束的音節仍然送出字。要送出「你好」，請從候選選取，或打上聲調。
