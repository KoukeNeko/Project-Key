## Changes

↩️ Enter sends the last syllable as Zhuyin when you haven't typed its tone, because that Zhuyin is one of its candidates. Typing ㄋㄧㄏㄠ and then Enter gives 你ㄏㄠ; syllables a later key already closed still go as words. To send 你好, pick it from the candidates or type the tone.

## 變更

↩️ 按 Enter 時，最後一個還沒打聲調的音節會以注音符號送出，因為注音本身也是它的候選之一。例如打 ㄋㄧㄏㄠ 再按 Enter，會送出「你ㄏㄠ」；被後面按鍵結束的音節仍然送出字。要送出「你好」，從候選中選取，或打上聲調。
