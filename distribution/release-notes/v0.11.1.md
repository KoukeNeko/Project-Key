## Bug fixes

- **Candidate window in the wrong app (Linux).** On Wayland compositors built on Smithay, such as COSMIC and niri, the candidate window could stay in the app where it first appeared, after the 中/英 badge or the Tab hint had been shown. It now stays at the caret of the app being typed in.

## 繁體中文

## 錯誤修正

- **候選視窗出現在其他 App（Linux）**：在 COSMIC、niri 等以 Smithay 實作的 Wayland 合成器上，顯示過中英模式提示或 Tab 提示後，候選視窗可能停留在最初出現的 App 中。現在候選視窗會跟著正在輸入的 App 的游標。
